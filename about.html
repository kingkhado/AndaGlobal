Our story

22 years building infrastructure South Africa relies on

From Makhado Projects in 2002 to AndaGlobal today — a total-solutions civil construction partner driven by engineering excellence and community impact.

Our backstory

A total-solutions construction company

Founded in 2002, formerly known as Makhado Projects, Anda Global Management delivers all-encompassing civil construction management — from pre-construction to handover. With 22+ years of hands-on field experience, fast-tracking takes centre stage while we fulfil your needs on budget and on time.

Guided by a commitment to lead in Civil Construction, General Building and Infrastructure Development, our strategy and experience equip us for sustainable success. We bring an ultra-modern, customer-centric and results-oriented definition to the industry — tailored to every project, whatever its scale.

Our mission

Build, create & innovate as if it's our own

With every project we build as if it were our very own. Anda Global lays the foundation for an ultra-modern definition of civil construction — designed entirely around clients, result-oriented, and offering personalised solutions for every project, large or small. Our mission is a hassle-free, one-stop construction solution at fair rates.

Our vision

Showcasing African engineering to the world

We aim to drive economic empowerment and act as a catalyst for change — creating opportunities, promoting inclusivity and uplifting communities. Through partnerships, support for local businesses and training, we empower people and demonstrate the immense potential of African engineering skills across South Africa and beyond.
